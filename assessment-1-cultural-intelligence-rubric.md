## [KS1.2] Technical and Technological Knowledge

**Points: 17**

### High Distinction: 17 to >14.44 pts

You demonstrate an exceptional ability to apply technical and digital capabilities, including the appropriate and responsible use of generative AI or other digital technologies, to interpret and analyse your CQ T1 assessment data. You critically evaluate the accuracy, relevance, strengths, and limitations of AI-generated outputs, using evidence from your CQ profile and personal experiences to justify which recommendations you would adopt, modify, or reject. You demonstrate sophisticated data interpretation and digital literacy by integrating AI-generated insights with your own critical judgement to develop well-informed, evidence-based strategies for your ongoing CQ development. You demonstrate a strong understanding of the opportunities, limitations, and ethical considerations associated with using emerging technologies to support your professional learning and development. Where appropriate, you integrate high quality, relevant and credible references to strengthen your discussion.

### Distinction: 14.44 to >12.74 pts

You demonstrate a high level of ability to apply technical and digital capabilities, including the appropriate use of generative AI or other digital technologies, to interpret and analyse your CQ T1 assessment data. You critically evaluate the AI-generated outputs, identifying their relevance, strengths, and limitations, and justify most recommendations you would adopt, modify, or reject using evidence from your CQ profile and personal experiences. You effectively integrate AI-generated insights with your own judgement to develop appropriate strategies for your ongoing CQ development. You demonstrate a clear understanding of the opportunities, limitations, and ethical considerations associated with using emerging technologies to support professional learning. Where appropriate, you integrate highly relevant and credible references that effectively support your discussion.

### Credit: 12.74 to >11.04 pts

You demonstrate a sound ability to apply technical and digital capabilities to interpret your CQ T1 assessment data using generative AI or another digital technology. You evaluate the AI-generated outputs and discuss their usefulness in relation to your CQ profile, although the evaluation may lack depth or critical insight. You justify recommendations using some evidence from your CQ results and personal experiences and develop appropriate strategies for your CQ development. You demonstrate a sound understanding of the opportunities and limitations of using emerging technologies to support professional learning. Where appropriate, you use relevant references to support your discussion, although the integration or application of sources may be inconsistent.

### Pass: 11.04 to >8.49 pts

You demonstrate a satisfactory ability to use generative AI or another digital technology to interpret your CQ T1 assessment data. You describe the AI-generated recommendations and provide some evaluation of their usefulness, although the discussion is largely descriptive rather than critical. Limited justification is provided for the recommendations you would adopt, modify, or reject. You demonstrate a basic understanding of how digital technologies can support CQ development, with limited consideration of their limitations or ethical implications. Where appropriate, you include some references; however, they may be of variable relevance or credibility, or are not consistently integrated into the discussion.

### Marginal Fail: 8.49 to >6.79 pts

You demonstrate limited ability to apply technical and digital capabilities to interpret your CQ T1 assessment data. There is little or no critical evaluation of the AI-generated outputs, and recommendations are accepted or rejected without adequate justification. Discussion demonstrates limited understanding of how digital technologies can support CQ development, with minimal or no consideration of their limitations, ethical implications, or application to professional learning. References are absent, inappropriate, or insufficient, and do not support the discussion where required.

### Fail: 6.79 to >4.24 pts

You demonstrate very limited ability to apply technical and digital capabilities to interpret your CQ T1 assessment data. There is minimal or no evidence of appropriate use of generative AI or another digital technology, or the technology is used incorrectly or inappropriately. The AI-generated outputs are largely described rather than evaluated, with little or no justification for the recommendations you would adopt, modify, or reject. The discussion demonstrates very limited understanding of how digital technologies can support CQ development and shows little awareness of their opportunities, limitations, or ethical considerations. References are absent or inappropriate where required and do not support the discussion.

### Low Fail: 4.24 to >0 pts

Your work provides little evidence of learning in relation to this criterion.

### NE: 0 pts

No evidence provided.

## [PC3.1] Written Communication

**Points: 6**

### High Distinction: 6 to >5.1 pts

Your reflection is exceptionally well written and presented to a professional standard. It is logically organised, coherent, and engaging, with a clear and consistent reflective and authorial voice throughout. Your ideas are communicated clearly and effectively, with seamless transitions between sections. There are no errors in grammar, spelling, punctuation, or vocabulary. Where appropriate, sources are accurately acknowledged using the required referencing style, with a complete and correctly formatted reference list. Your reflection adheres to the prescribed word count.

### Distinction: 5.1 to >4.5 pts

Your reflection is very well written and presented to a high professional standard. It is well organised, coherent, and easy to follow, with a clear and consistent reflective and authorial voice throughout. Your ideas are communicated effectively, with logical transitions between sections. There are very few minor errors in grammar, spelling, punctuation, or vocabulary that do not affect readability. Where appropriate, sources are accurately acknowledged using the required referencing style, with a complete and appropriately formatted reference list. Your reflection adheres to the prescribed word count.

### Credit: 4.5 to >3.9 pts

Your reflection is well written and appropriately presented. It is logically organised, with a generally consistent reflective and authorial voice. Ideas are communicated clearly, although transitions between sections may occasionally lack flow or cohesion. Minor errors in grammar, spelling, punctuation, or vocabulary are evident but do not significantly affect meaning. Where appropriate, sources are acknowledged using the required referencing style, although some referencing or formatting errors may be present. Your reflection adheres to the prescribed word count.

### Pass: 3.9 to >3 pts

Your reflection is adequately written and presented, with an identifiable structure and reflective voice. The organisation is generally logical, although some sections may lack clarity or cohesion. Ideas are communicated with satisfactory clarity; however, expression may be repetitive or occasionally unclear. Errors in grammar, spelling, punctuation, or vocabulary are noticeable but generally do not impede understanding. Where appropriate, some sources are acknowledged, although inconsistencies or errors in referencing and formatting are evident. Your reflection is within, or only marginally outside, the prescribed word count.

### Marginal Fail: 3 to >2.4 pts

Your reflection demonstrates limited written communication and organisation. The structure is inconsistent or difficult to follow, and the reflective and authorial voice is underdeveloped. Ideas are communicated unclearly, with limited cohesion between sections. Frequent errors in grammar, spelling, punctuation, and vocabulary detract from readability and understanding. Referencing is incomplete, inaccurate, or inconsistent where required, and the reference list contains significant errors or omissions. The reflection does not adhere to the prescribed word count.

### Fail: 2.4 to >1.5 pts

Your reflection is poorly written and lacks clear organisation, coherence, and a reflective voice. Ideas are difficult to understand due to persistent errors in grammar, spelling, punctuation, vocabulary, and sentence structure. There is little or no logical progression between sections. Referencing is absent or largely incorrect where required, and a reference list is missing or does not follow the required style. The reflection is substantially outside the prescribed word count.

### Low Fail: 1.5 to >0 pts

Your work provides little evidence of learning in relation to this criterion.

### NE: 0 pts

No evidence provided.

## [TS4.1] Self-Reflection, Responsibility and Accountability

**Points: 17**

### High Distinction: 17 to >14.44 pts

You demonstrate an exceptional level of critical reflection on your intercultural experiences and self-awareness, thoughtfully examining how your perceptions, behaviours, and responses to culturally diverse situations have evolved over time. You provide a perceptive and insightful interpretation of your CQ T1 assessment results, critically analysing your strengths, areas for development, and the implications for your personal and professional practice. Your reflection is enriched by meaningful and relevant examples from your own experiences, demonstrating a sophisticated understanding of your intercultural capabilities, personal growth, and ongoing development. You present a comprehensive, evidence-informed, and well-justified CQ development plan that includes clearly articulated short-term and long-term SMART goals aligned with relevant CQ sub-dimensions. Your proposed action steps are specific, realistic, measurable, and achievable, demonstrating a strong commitment to continuous CQ development and effective engagement in culturally diverse business and social contexts.

### Distinction: 14.44 to >12.74 pts

You demonstrate a high level of critical reflection on your intercultural experiences and self-awareness, clearly examining how your perceptions, behaviours, and responses to culturally diverse situations have developed over time. You provide a well-reasoned interpretation of your CQ T1 assessment results, analysing your strengths, areas for development, and the implications for your personal and professional practice. Your reflection is supported by relevant examples from your own experiences, demonstrating a strong understanding of your intercultural capabilities and ongoing development. You present a well-developed and justified CQ development plan that includes clearly articulated short-term and long-term SMART goals aligned with relevant CQ sub-dimensions. Your proposed action steps are realistic, measurable, and demonstrate a clear commitment to enhancing your Cultural Intelligence.

### Credit: 12.74 to >11.04 pts

You demonstrate a sound level of critical reflection on your intercultural experiences and self-awareness, examining how your perceptions, behaviours, and responses to culturally diverse situations have changed over time. You provide a clear interpretation of your CQ T1 assessment results, discussing your strengths, areas for development, and their implications for your personal and professional practice. Your reflection includes relevant examples from your own experiences, demonstrating a sound understanding of your intercultural capabilities. You present an appropriate CQ development plan that includes short-term and long-term SMART goals aligned with relevant CQ sub-dimensions. Your proposed action steps are generally realistic and demonstrate an intention to continue developing your Cultural Intelligence.

### Pass: 11.04 to >8.49 pts

You demonstrate a satisfactory level of reflection on your intercultural experiences and self-awareness, identifying some changes in your perceptions, behaviours, or responses to culturally diverse situations. You provide a basic interpretation of your CQ T1 assessment results by identifying strengths and areas for development, although the analysis is largely descriptive rather than critical. Your reflection includes some examples from your own experiences, demonstrating a developing understanding of your intercultural capabilities. You present a CQ development plan that includes short-term and long-term goals, although the SMART criteria may not be fully addressed or clearly linked to relevant CQ sub-dimensions. Your proposed action steps are appropriate but lack detail or specificity.

### Marginal Fail: 8.49 to >6.79 pts

You demonstrate limited reflection on your intercultural experiences and self-awareness, with minimal consideration of how your perceptions, behaviours, or responses to culturally diverse situations have developed. You provide a limited interpretation of your CQ T1 assessment results, with little analysis of your strengths, areas for development, or their implications for your personal and professional practice. Examples from your own experiences are limited, superficial, or not clearly connected to your discussion. Your CQ development plan is underdeveloped, with goals that are vague, incomplete, or not aligned with relevant CQ sub-dimensions or the SMART framework. The proposed action steps lack sufficient detail or feasibility.

### Fail: 6.79 to >4.24 pts

You demonstrate very limited or no meaningful reflection on your intercultural experiences or self-awareness. There is little or no interpretation of your CQ T1 assessment results, and strengths, areas for development, and their implications are largely absent or inaccurate. Reflection is descriptive, unsupported by relevant personal examples, and demonstrates minimal understanding of Cultural Intelligence or personal development. The CQ development plan is absent or incomplete, with goals that do not meet the SMART criteria and are not aligned with relevant CQ sub-dimensions. Proposed action steps are unclear, unrealistic, or absent.

### Low Fail: 4.24 to >0 pts

Your work provides little evidence of learning in relation to this criterion.
